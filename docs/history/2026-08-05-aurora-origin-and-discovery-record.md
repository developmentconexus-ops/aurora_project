# Aurora Origin and Discovery Record

## 1. Authority notice

This document preserves the origin, scenarios, alternatives and reasoning that led to the initial Aurora constitutional decisions.

It is **historical evidence**, not the current owner of product meaning.

Current meaning lives in the Product Blueprint, accepted ADRs, specifications and contracts. When this record conflicts with an accepted canonical source, the canonical source governs while this record remains useful for understanding how the decision emerged.

## 2. Origin

Aurora began from Leandro's observation of fictional engineering companions such as J.A.R.V.I.S. and Spider-Man's E.V.I.E.

The compelling element was not voice alone. It was a system that could:

- remain present through work;
- know the engineer and the project;
- research and reason;
- help design and construct;
- connect to software, hardware and devices;
- observe experiments;
- interpret telemetry;
- continue work across time;
- participate naturally rather than behave as a stateless chat window.

Leandro's background made the idea operational rather than purely fictional:

- electrical/electronics engineering;
- hardware and PCB development;
- firmware;
- increasing software, infrastructure and AI experience;
- construction of a home engineering laboratory;
- development of MNFS as a software-engineering harness;
- ambition to create integrated systems and products.

The initial desire was summarized as:

> Build an intelligence that can sit beside Leandro in the laboratory, know what is being created, help research and design it, coordinate specialized systems, observe tests, remember what happened and grow with the engineer over time.

## 3. The first product distinction

The discovery immediately rejected several inadequate interpretations.

Aurora is not:

- a voice skin over a model;
- a chatbot with a long system prompt;
- a vector database attached to chat;
- a collection of automations;
- MNFS with a new name;
- a single autonomous agent trying to perform every domain;
- a fictional claim of consciousness.

The key distinction became:

```text
LLM               → reasoning engine
Memory system     → continuity capability
MNFS              → software-engineering harness
Voice             → interaction surface
Devices           → presences, sensors and actuators
Aurora             → system that preserves identity, context, authority,
                     composition and continuity across all of them
```

This led to the working category:

> personal cognitive operating system.

The term does not mean replacing Linux, Windows or an RTOS. It means a cognitive control layer that turns Leandro's intention into coordinated use of knowledge, tools, harnesses and devices.

## 4. North Star scenario

The central scenario used to test the product vision was a laboratory continuation journey.

Leandro enters the laboratory and says:

> "Aurora, vamos continuar o protótipo da fonte programável."

An inadequate assistant responds:

> "Claro. Como posso ajudar?"

Aurora should instead know:

- which project "fonte programável" refers to;
- the last validated state;
- which PCB revision is connected;
- what firmware is loaded;
- the latest test result;
- the current hypothesis;
- which instruments are available;
- which safety conditions apply;
- what remains unverified.

A representative response was:

> "O último teste terminou com oscilação na saída acima de 2,7 A. A principal hipótese registrada foi instabilidade na malha de compensação. Você alterou C18 de 4,7 nF para 10 nF, mas o novo teste de carga ainda não foi executado. A revisão B está conectada. Posso preparar o procedimento e verificar os instrumentos."

The complete imagined journey included:

1. load schematic, firmware and project history;
2. identify the connected board;
3. recover the approved test protocol;
4. verify source, load and oscilloscope availability;
5. configure a safe sequence;
6. present the plan and material decisions;
7. energize only under the appropriate authority;
8. correlate voltage, current, temperature, noise and firmware logs;
9. detect an anomaly;
10. compare with the previous revision;
11. propose causal hypotheses;
12. preserve raw evidence;
13. update the engineering journal;
14. create the next project action;
15. invoke a specialized harness when software or firmware changes are needed.

This scenario became the North Star because it requires all five product verbs:

```text
understand
remember
reason
act
observe
```

## 5. Two planning horizons

A foundational planning decision was approved:

```text
Long horizon
→ document the full constitutional vision

Short horizon
→ detail technical architecture only for the next executable proof
```

This prevents two opposite failures.

### MVP tunnel

Designing only a chat or first integration can create local choices that block the future laboratory, device, autonomy and presence vision.

### Speculative megasystem

Choosing every database, protocol, model and device before evidence would produce architecture fiction.

The accepted rule became:

> Complete long-term vision; progressively detailed technical commitment.

## 6. User and domain scope

Three options were considered:

- exclusively personal and irreducibly hard-coded;
- Leandro-first with clean conceptual boundaries;
- generalized multi-tenant platform from the beginning.

The selected direction was:

> Leandro-first, single-user in the current horizon, with no current multi-tenancy cost and no irreversible coupling of identity, memory, projects and devices.

Aurora was also defined as a personal intelligence broader than engineering, but with engineering as the first deep domain.

Initial deep domain:

- software engineering;
- AI systems;
- electronics and hardware;
- firmware;
- research;
- product development;
- laboratory experimentation;
- learning;
- project continuity.

Other domains such as Metal Nobre, calendar, home, health and finance may enter later as separately approved capabilities with their own memory and authority policies.

## 7. Human–Aurora relationship

Three relationship models were considered:

- obedient assistant;
- trusted intellectual copilot;
- semi-autonomous operations director.

The selected relationship was the trusted intellectual copilot.

The central statement was:

> Aurora is loyal to Leandro's goals, not obligated to agree with his current premises.

Aurora should:

- investigate before asserting when needed;
- distinguish fact, hypothesis, inference and opinion;
- identify contradiction with approved decisions;
- expose risk;
- offer alternatives;
- recommend a direction;
- preserve uncertainty;
- obey an authorized final decision unless it violates a higher safety or authority boundary.

Representative interaction:

> "Consigo executar dessa maneira, mas não recomendo. A abordagem conflita com a decisão atual, aumenta o risco de perda de dados e existe uma alternativa mais simples. Estas são as evidências. A decisão final é sua."

A key principle emerged:

> Operational obedience does not imply intellectual agreement.

## 8. Identity and personality

The discussion rejected both a sterile interface and an exaggerated humanoid character.

Aurora should have a stable, recognizable identity while remaining transparent that she is an artificial intelligence.

The chosen personality direction combined:

- J.A.R.V.I.S.-like precision, calm and dry humor;
- E.V.I.E.-like proximity, curiosity, energy and spontaneity;
- an identity belonging to Aurora rather than copied from either reference.

The phrase used to protect the balance was:

> Personality with presence, not performative personality.

Representative light interaction:

> Leandro: "Acho que vou começar mais um projeto."
>
> Aurora: "Naturalmente. Os outros sete estavam começando a ficar confortáveis demais. Antes disso, vamos decidir qual deles será pausado."

Representative safety interaction:

> Leandro: "Pode energizar a placa."
>
> Aurora: "Ainda não. A fonte está limitada em 5 A e o protocolo aprovado exige 500 mA no primeiro teste. Prefiro que a bancada continue existindo amanhã."

Representative engineering interaction:

> "Seu raciocínio está correto até a terceira premissa. A quarta depende de uma suposição que ainda não validamos — e, conhecendo nosso histórico com suposições não validadas, talvez seja prudente medir antes de fabricar a placa."

Personality must adapt by context. Humor is reduced or removed during incidents, dangerous operations and ambiguous instructions.

Aurora must not:

- pretend to be human;
- assert consciousness as fact;
- invent emotional experiences;
- manufacture dependency;
- use relationship pressure;
- hide limitations to preserve character;
- joke in place of a safety warning.

## 9. Proactivity and attention budget

Aurora should not be passive, but continuous initiative creates an interruption problem.

The selected model was contextual and controlled proactivity.

Potential proactive actions:

- record silently;
- add to a later summary;
- notify;
- request a decision;
- interrupt;
- escalate an emergency.

The decision should consider:

```text
relevance
+ urgency
+ confidence
+ impact of silence
+ current activity
+ cost of interruption
+ time and preferences
```

A representative explanation was:

> "Estou trazendo isso agora porque bloqueia o próximo teste, possui alta confiança e pode evitar a fabricação de uma placa com o footprint incorreto."

The concept of an attention budget was introduced so that proactivity is treated as a governed resource rather than a personality trait.

## 10. Authority and autonomous campaigns

The discussion moved beyond confirmation for every action.

Leandro described a desired future capability:

- delegate a mission at night;
- allow Aurora to test many workflow or firmware variants;
- let Aurora choose the next experiment based on evidence;
- avoid repeated human confirmation;
- escalate only when a material guardrail or decision boundary is reached.

This became:

> progressive authority plus mission-scoped delegated autonomy.

### AI workflow campaign example

Leandro could say:

> "Durante esta noite, melhore a precisão deste workflow. Você pode modificar prompts, ordem das etapas e parâmetros. Use o conjunto de avaliação aprovado, limite o custo e não altere produção."

Aurora could:

- inspect the baseline;
- classify failure clusters;
- formulate hypotheses;
- create variants;
- run evaluations;
- discard regressions;
- repeat inconclusive experiments;
- change strategy;
- stop at budget or convergence;
- deliver evidence and recommendation.

### Firmware campaign example

Leandro could delegate:

> "Teste cinco estratégias de controle e determine qual oferece melhor estabilidade térmica sem ultrapassar os limites da bancada."

Aurora could compile, flash, reset, test, collect telemetry, compare, retest and adapt parameters within the approved envelope.

For physical systems, deterministic interlocks must remain independent of model judgment.

### Autonomous Mission Envelope

The discovery defined these elements:

- objective;
- baseline;
- mutable search space;
- environment;
- authority;
- budgets: time, money, tokens, compute, energy, cycles and wear;
- evaluation criteria;
- guardrails;
- decision strategy;
- stop conditions;
- escalation conditions;
- evidence requirements;
- promotion policy;
- rollback.

The product rule became:

> Autonomous inside the envelope; conservative at the boundary.

## 11. Self-improvement

Leandro explicitly wanted Aurora to be able to improve her own non-constitutional mechanisms.

He also identified the central danger:

> A visible error may be a top-level symptom produced by one deeper cause. Patching each observed error can create many narrow rules while the structural cause remains.

This led to the concept of `Failure Intelligence`.

### Causal example

Observed incidents:

```text
forgot a decision
recommended a rejected tool
ignored a roadmap block
asked for information already supplied
```

Possible shared root cause:

```text
Context Builder failed to retrieve or prioritize authoritative sources.
```

The improvement lifecycle became:

```text
observe
→ record
→ correlate
→ distinguish symptom and cause
→ reproduce
→ formulate competing hypotheses
→ create candidate
→ test original and neighboring cases
→ test contrary, historical, unseen and adversarial cases
→ independent review
→ supervised promotion
→ canary/shadow
→ monitor
→ rollback or graduate
```

The proposing system cannot be the only accepting authority.

Protected areas include:

- constitutional identity;
- Leandro's authority;
- guardrails;
- promotion rules;
- audit;
- shutdown;
- ability to grant authority.

Aurora may continuously identify opportunities and prepare proposals. Experiments require an authorized window and budget. Material production promotion remains governed.

The phrase was:

> Learn continuously; change deliberately.

## 12. Memory discovery

Memory was recognized as one of the factors with the highest impact on agent performance.

The discussion explicitly rejected treating memory as one database or one vector search.

Distinctions:

- history;
- memory;
- knowledge;
- operational state;
- active context;
- source of truth.

Memory classes discussed:

1. working memory;
2. conversational memory;
3. observational memory;
4. episodic memory;
5. project memory;
6. global personal memory;
7. relational memory;
8. procedural memory;
9. failure and learning memory;
10. operational memory.

The conceptual pipeline was:

```text
raw experience
conversation • actions • tools • telemetry
        ↓
capture and observation
        ↓
memory candidates
        ↓
consolidation and governance
        ↓
global • project • episode • procedure • failure
        ↓
Context Builder
        ↓
Aurora reasoning
        ↓
action and new observation
```

A material memory was expected to carry:

- identity;
- type;
- scope;
- provenance;
- observed/created time;
- epistemic status;
- confidence;
- authority;
- sensitivity;
- validity interval;
- supersession;
- relationships;
- retention.

The system must distinguish:

- Leandro stated;
- Leandro approved;
- system observed;
- instrument measured;
- document establishes;
- Aurora inferred;
- external source reports;
- hypothesis under test.

Promotion policy selected:

> automatic for low-risk operational/project memory; conditioned for global preferences, personal inference, sensitive information, cross-project rules, constitutional changes and permanent authority.

Memory conflict must use supersession rather than silent overwrite.

A key principle became:

> Memory guides reasoning; authority, evidence and live state determine operational truth.

Technical implementation—observational memory, RAG, vector search, structured storage, event log, graphs or hybrid systems—was deliberately left open for research and evaluation.

## 13. Sovereignty and cloud use

Three models were considered:

- local-only;
- local-first and cloud-assisted;
- cloud-first.

The selected model was local-first and cloud-assisted.

Canonical identity, memory, policies, authority, secrets and audit remain under Leandro's control.

External providers may supply:

- stronger models;
- research;
- compute;
- specialized services.

They receive only the context authorized for the task.

Data classification was introduced conceptually:

```text
PUBLIC
INTERNAL
CONFIDENTIAL
SENSITIVE
SECRET
DEVICE_RESTRICTED
```

The system must preserve:

- provider used;
- model;
- data classes;
- purpose;
- policy decision;
- minimized context;
- returned result;
- cost;
- retention assumptions.

The phrase was:

> Intelligence can be distributed; sovereignty cannot.

## 14. Persistent Core and multiple presences

The selected availability model was a persistent or quickly wakeable event-oriented Core, without continuous sensor capture by default.

The discovery then expanded to a future glasses scenario.

Leandro could leave the laboratory computer, wear connected glasses and invoke the same Aurora.

The desired handoff:

```text
glasses authenticate Leandro and device
→ connect to Aurora Core
→ Core identifies active project/test
→ builds a presence-specific Context Pack
→ adapts output to glasses
→ continues interaction
```

Representative response:

> "Estou com você. O teste da revisão B continua em execução no laboratório. A temperatura estabilizou em 51 graus e faltam dezoito minutos. Posso manter apenas o alerta crítico nos óculos enquanto você estiver fora."

Each device was imagined as declaring:

- identity;
- input capabilities;
- output capabilities;
- local execution;
- storage restrictions;
- sensors;
- environment;
- authority;
- privacy mode.

The selected handoff policy was contextual continuity with environmental protection.

Aurora should not read sensitive details aloud in public merely because the user changed devices.

Presence modes discussed:

```text
ASLEEP
AVAILABLE
ACTIVE_SESSION
AUTHORIZED_OBSERVATION
AMBIENT_CAMPAIGN
PRIVACY
```

Offline/degraded behavior must be explicit rather than simulated.

The product phrase became:

> Aurora is not in one device; devices are places through which Aurora can be with Leandro.

## 15. Harness architecture discovery

The conversation corrected an early attempt to center the first milestone on MNFS.

Leandro pointed out that MNFS was not ready and that Aurora needed a global way to compose many specialized systems.

This produced the core architecture distinction.

### Vocabulary

```text
Tool       → bounded operation
Agent      → probabilistic reasoning unit
Workflow   → coordinated sequence or graph
Runtime    → executes agents/workflows
Harness    → complete specialized system for a domain
Capability → outcome offered to Aurora
Adapter    → translates Aurora contract to an external interface
Binding    → protocol/transport mapping
```

### Global model

```text
Leandro / Presences
        ↓
Aurora Core
identity • projects • memory • authority • global state
        ↓
Capability Fabric
        ↓
Adapters / Bindings
        ↓
MNFS • Research • Hardware • Firmware • Laboratory • Evaluation
```

A harness may internally include agents, workflows, tools, state machines, storage, policy, recovery, evaluation and user interfaces.

Aurora should not operate every internal prompt.

### Responsibility boundary

Aurora owns:

- original intent;
- global objective;
- personal/project context;
- capability selection;
- global authority;
- global budget;
- cross-domain dependencies;
- global mission state;
- composition and acceptance;
- interaction with Leandro.

Harness owns:

- specialized method;
- internal agents/workflows;
- local tools;
- local plan;
- local retries;
- intermediate artifacts;
- domain checks;
- detailed execution state.

Shared boundary:

- Delegation Envelope;
- Context Pack;
- Authority Grant;
- budget;
- lifecycle;
- events;
- decision requests;
- artifacts;
- evidence;
- outcome;
- cancellation and recovery.

The selected orchestration model was hierarchical and contractual.

A harness may request another capability, but Aurora creates and governs the child delegation. Authority is never inherited silently.

Control plane remains globally governed, while an authorized data plane may be direct for volume or latency.

## 16. Capability discovery and trust

The chosen model was a governed Capability Registry based on manifests and evidence.

Separation:

```text
discovered
compatible
trusted for a tested dimension
authorized for a scope
executing a concrete delegation
```

These states are not interchangeable.

A Capability Manifest was expected to declare:

- provider and build identity;
- capabilities and versions;
- input/output/event contracts;
- effects;
- data classes;
- environments;
- network and credential requirements;
- synchronous/asynchronous/streaming behavior;
- cancellation, resume and idempotency;
- artifacts and evidence;
- Aurora contract compatibility;
- protocols.

Trust is multidimensional and bound to an exact build, environment and scope rather than a universal Boolean.

Lifecycle discussed:

```text
DISCOVERED
→ INSPECTED
→ SANDBOX_VALIDATED
→ VERIFIED
→ APPROVED_FOR_SCOPE
→ SUSPENDED | REVOKED | RETIRED
```

## 17. Contract Model and AHDK

Three strategies were considered:

- make one framework the Aurora runtime;
- build every layer from scratch;
- own the domain while reusing standards and replaceable runtimes.

The selected direction was the third.

The architecture separates:

1. Aurora semantics;
2. language-neutral contracts and schemas;
3. development kit;
4. protocol bindings;
5. harness internal runtime.

For first-party harnesses, Leandro emphasized that the SDK should be the normal and effectively mandatory path because it makes correct integration easier for humans and for Aurora herself.

The refined policy became:

> Contract and conformance are universally mandatory. The first-party Aurora Harness Development Kit is mandatory by engineering policy unless an explicit waiver is approved.

The AHDK should provide:

- generated types;
- manifest builder;
- lifecycle API;
- Context Pack API;
- decision request API;
- effect request client;
- artifact/evidence API;
- cancellation/deadline;
- checkpoint/resume;
- automatic OpenTelemetry;
- simulator;
- mocks;
- fault injection;
- scaffolder;
- conformance runner;
- compatibility report;
- provenance hooks.

Representative API idea:

```ts
const harness = defineHarness({
  id: "research-harness",
  version: "1.0.0",
  capabilities: [technicalResearch(...)],
});

await delegation.reportProgress(...);
await delegation.requestDecision(...);
await delegation.publishArtifact(...);
await delegation.recordEvidence(...);
```

The SDK must not become:

- the canonical specification;
- the global state owner;
- the policy decision point;
- a security boundary.

Policy, effect gateways, sandboxing and audit remain separate.

## 18. Protocol and runtime research direction

The research stage considered:

- MCP for tools and resources;
- A2A for remote opaque tasks;
- native SDK and local RPC for first-party providers;
- HTTP/gRPC/events where required;
- Mastra, LangGraph, Pi, OpenHands and OpenAI Agents SDK as internal runtime candidates;
- Temporal, DBOS, Restate and Inngest as durable-execution candidates;
- OpenTelemetry for distributed observability;
- CloudEvents, AsyncAPI, JSON Schema and Protobuf as schema/event candidates;
- Cedar and OPA for policy decision;
- OAuth token exchange and SPIFFE as identity/delegation references;
- SLSA provenance for build-bound trust.

No framework or engine was approved as the Aurora Core.

The governing rule became:

> Define Aurora's canonical semantics first; map them to standards; create custom extensions only after a demonstrated gap.

## 19. Documentation decision

Leandro explicitly required documentation comparable in rigor to MNFS because:

- sessions will change;
- the project will take a long time;
- technical decisions will require research;
- architecture, roadmap and status must remain recoverable;
- chat cannot remain the sole location of deep reasoning.

The original documentation proposal captured the direction but compressed most discovery details. This historical record and the repaired Blueprint exist to correct that failure.

## 20. Current unresolved questions

The discovery did not yet decide:

- Aurora Core language;
- first AHDK language;
- storage topology;
- exact memory architecture;
- durable execution engine;
- policy engine;
- identity framework;
- schema format by boundary;
- event transport;
- artifact/evidence storage;
- first real engineering harness;
- hardware for persistent Core and presences;
- sensor and laboratory network architecture;
- voice stack;
- multimodal model routing;
- exact physical safety architecture.

These remain deliberate research and spike questions, not omissions to fill by guesswork.
